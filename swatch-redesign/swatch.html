    Swatch tips

    Printing 🖨️

    💯
    Keep Scale at 100%In the print screen, turn off "Fit to page" so every box prints at its real size.
    📃
    Use thick, smooth paperMarker paper or 200gsm+ cardstock stops alcohol ink bleeding into the next box.
    💾
    No printer nearby?Pick "Save as PDF" in the print screen and print it later at a copy shop.

    Swatching 🖍️

    🌗
    Swatch both nibsFill the box with the brush tip, then add a stripe with the chisel so you see both.
    🎨
    Layer for depthTwo coats show the darkest value. Big boxes have a dashed blend test box for that.
    ✏️
    Blank or pre-filledScreens can't show ink exactly, so print blank to swatch your real markers for the truest chart.
    ✂️
    Cards for the deskThe Cards layout cuts into one card per color family, easy to keep beside each marker case.
