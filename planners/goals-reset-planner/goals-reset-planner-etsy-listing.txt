DESCRIPTION (copy everything below this line)
============================================================
The 2027 goal planner that keeps you going past February. Turn big goals into weekly steps, see if you're ahead or behind pace, and check in for 5 minutes every Sunday.

Please note: this is an interactive app that opens in your web browser (phone, tablet or computer). It is not a printable PDF, a spreadsheet or a GoodNotes file.

WHY IT HELPS
Most resolutions fade by February because a goal written once in January never turns into a next step. This planner breaks each goal into small steps, shows your progress on a journey path, tells you if you're on pace, and gives you a calm weekly check-in so you never lose the thread.

★ WHAT'S INSIDE

TODAY
• How many goals are on track, and your next step
• Before your year begins: a countdown and your reset checklist
• This week's top 3 steps, checked off in one tap
• A week row that fills in every day you take a step
• Today's habits with streaks
• Life area tiles showing progress for health, money, career and more

GOALS
• Goals in 8 life areas: health, money, career, relationships, growth, fun, home and mind
• Measure each goal your way: a checklist of steps, a number (save $5,000, read 24 books) or a habit (meditate 100 times)
• Every goal shows its progress and whether you're ahead, on track or behind pace for its target date
• A journey path for each goal, with every step as a stop on the road to the trophy
• Your "why", written where you'll see it on hard days
• Progress log for number goals with quick amounts
• A full celebration screen when you finish a goal

HABITS
• Daily check-offs with streaks
• A 5-week heatmap for each habit
• Link a habit to a goal and every check-in counts toward it

REVIEW
• Sunday check-in: rate your week, write your wins and what got in the way, then set next week's top 3
• Monthly review with steps by week, progress on every goal and a monthly reflection
• Year at a glance: all 12 months, goals completed and check-ins

RESET
• Year in review: 10 guided reflection questions and your top 3 wins of last year
• Wheel of life: score 8 areas from 1 to 10 on a radar chart and compare it with last time
• Word of the year, with ideas to choose from
• Vision board with your own photos, dreams and quotes
• Bucket list with ideas to get you started
• A reset checklist that walks you through all of it

✓ FEATURES

• Light and dark mode (follows your phone automatically)
• Start any time: set your year to begin on January 1 or today
• No app to install, no account, no subscription, no ads

GOOD TO KNOW
• This is a digital product. Nothing is shipped.
• Your entries are saved in the browser on the device you use. To move to a new phone or computer, export a backup and import it there.
• Personal use only.

FAQ
Does it work on iPhone?
Yes. It opens in Safari or any browser.

Can I start in the middle of the year?
Yes. Set your start date to today in Settings and your 365 days count from there.

Do I need internet?
No. Once the file is open, it works offline.

Can I use it on two devices?
Yes. Each device keeps its own copy. Use Export backup and Import backup to move your data between them.

Is there a subscription?
Never. You pay once and it's yours.
