DESCRIPTION (copy everything below this line)
============================================================
A gentle weight loss tracker app for your phone. Check in with your weight, water and mood in about 10 seconds a day, then watch your real trend go down.

Please note: this is an interactive app that opens in your web browser (phone, tablet or computer). It is not a printable PDF, a spreadsheet or a GoodNotes file.

WHY IT HELPS
The scale jumps around every day, and that can make you want to quit. This tracker shows your trend weight, a smoothed line that ignores water-weight swings, so a "bad" morning stops feeling like failure. It's quick, kind and private: no ads, no account, no subscription, no shame.

★ WHAT'S INSIDE

TODAY
• Your trend weight, pounds lost and a progress ring to your goal
• A weekly streak track that lights up every day you log
• "Right now" card: the one thing to do next (check in, drink water, finish a habit)
• Quick log tiles: weigh in, water, food, movement, mood and wins in one tap
• Today's habits with streak counts

WEIGHT
• Weigh-in log with a smooth trend chart (30 days, 90 days or all time) and your goal line
• Plateau decoder: tells you when the scale went up but your trend is still going down
• Pace per week, projected goal date at your current pace, and BMI
• Start, now, goal and "to go" at a glance

FOOD
• Count calories and protein with a daily ring, or switch to Simple mode: rate meals Great, Okay or Treat with no counting at all
• Quick add: tap a recent food to log it again
• Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks, for today or any past day
• Water tracker with tap-to-fill glasses
• 14-day calorie chart with your target line

PROGRESS
• Body measurements for waist, hips, chest, thigh, arm and neck, with total inches lost and a mini chart for each area
• Progress photos with a drag-to-compare before-and-after slider (photos stay on your device)
• Non-scale victories timeline ("my jeans fit!") with quick ideas to get you started
• Milestone badges: every 5 lb (or 2 kg), 5% and 10% of your starting weight, halfway, goal weight, streaks and more, with a gentle celebration when you unlock one
• Insights: your week or month at a glance, including logging days, average calories and water, active minutes, your most reliable habit, protein and sleep patterns

MORE
• Movement log: walk, strength, yoga, cycle, dance or swim, with minutes and steps, and a weekly active-minutes goal
• Habit tracker: daily check-offs, streaks and a 5-week heatmap for each habit
• Meal planner: plan breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks for the week from your saved meals
• Grocery list: add ingredients from your meal plan in one tap, sorted by aisle
• Journal: mood faces, energy, hours of sleep and a daily prompt, plus a month of mood colors
• Settings: pounds or kilograms, your goal, height, and a starting calorie and protein estimate

✓ FEATURES
• Works on iPhone, Android, iPad, tablets, Mac and PC
• Light and dark mode (follows your phone automatically)
• Pounds and inches or kilograms and centimeters, switch anytime
• No app to install, no account, no subscription, no ads
• Private: everything is saved only on your device
• Works offline once it's open
• Backup and restore with one tap
• Undo if you delete something by mistake
• Sample data so you can explore every screen first

WHAT YOU RECEIVE
• Instant digital download: a zip with the tracker (.html file) and a 2-page Start Here guide (PDF)
• Open the zip, then open the .html file in your browser. The guide walks you through it on phone and computer, plus how to back up your data.

GOOD TO KNOW
• This is a digital product. Nothing is shipped.
• Your entries are saved in the browser on the device you use. To move to a new phone or computer, export a backup and import it there.
• Personal use only.
• This tracker helps you notice patterns and celebrate progress. It isn't medical advice, so please talk to your doctor before big changes to how you eat or move.

FAQ
Does it work on iPhone?
Yes. It opens in Safari or any browser, and the Start Here guide shows how to keep it handy.

Do I need internet?
No. Once the file is open, it works offline.

Can I use it on two devices?
Yes. Each device keeps its own copy. Use Export backup and Import backup to move your data between them.

Is there a subscription?
Never. You pay once and it's yours.
