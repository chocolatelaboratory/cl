DESCRIPTION
==================================================

A cheerful ADHD budget planner app that shows one number: what's safe to spend today. Bills, paydays, debt, envelopes and savings in one fun little app.

Please note: this is an interactive app that opens in your web browser on your phone, tablet or computer. It is not a printable PDF, a GoodNotes file or a spreadsheet.

Budgeting usually fails the same way: too many boxes, too much maths, and a guilty feeling every time you open it. This one is bright, bouncy and kind. Open it, see your number, log what you spent in two taps, and get on with your day. Tick off a bill and it throws a tiny party. Go over in an envelope and it just says "that's okay".

It never connects to your bank. You type the balance you see in your banking app, and the planner does the rest.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✨ WHAT'S INSIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

★ TODAY
• Safe to spend today: one big number, worked out from your balance, the bills still to come before payday and the days left until you get paid
• "How is this worked out?" shows every figure behind the number, so you can trust it
• One thing right now: a single next step (a bill due tomorrow, today's spending, a decision on something you parked). Do it, tap Done, the next one appears
• Quick log box: type "12 coffee" and it's logged in the right envelope
• Run to payday: a day-by-day strip to payday with your bill days and payday marked
• Today's spending list: tap any spend to fix or delete it (with Undo)
• Welcome back card: away for a week? One quick balance check and every number is right again. No lecture.

★ BILLS
• Every must-pay, soonest first, with a big check box to tick off (sparkles included)
• Still to go out before payday, this month's total and what's already paid
• Bills however yours really come: once a month, two days a month, every 2 weeks, every week, every few months, or just once
• Put 31 for "last day of the month" and it follows the month's length
• Big bills every few months (car insurance, yearly renewals): tick "put a bit aside each month" and the planner carries the monthly share, so the big one never wrecks a month and is never counted twice
• Debt minimums sit with your bills. Tick one and the debt balance comes down by itself

★ MONEY
• Spending envelopes: soft pastel tiles with monthly limits and progress bars. Rename them, add your own, change the emoji. Going over is a gentle note, never a red warning
• Paychecks: add each one with its own schedule (weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month, monthly). Two jobs or a side hustle? Add both, and it counts down to whichever lands next
• Paid early when payday is on a weekend: one switch
• Money in: log paychecks, refunds, gifts and side money
• Month calendar: bills, paydays and spending as colored dots. Tap any day to see what happened, plus what came in, what went out and what you kept

★ GOALS
• Debt-free road: a winding path with a stop for each debt and a trophy at the end
• Choose your payoff order: smallest first (snowball) or highest rate first (avalanche)
• Record extra payments and see a history of every one
• Savings goals with progress bars and quick +$10 / +$25 / +$50 / +$100 buttons
• Trophy screen with confetti when a debt is paid off or a goal is reached
• Wait list: want something right now? Park it for 48 hours. Then skip it (and watch "saved by skipping" grow) or buy it guilt-free
