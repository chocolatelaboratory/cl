Opening it on your device

On a computer

  1. Unzip the download (double-click it).
  2. Double-click ADHD Budget Planner.html. It opens in your web browser.
  3. Bookmark it (Ctrl+D on Windows, Cmd+D on Mac) so it is one click away.

On a phone or tablet

  1. Save the download to your phone and unzip it in your Files app.
  2. Open ADHD Budget Planner.html in your web browser.
  3. Open it the same way every time, so you always see your saved planner.
Tip: Keep the file somewhere easy to find, like your Documents or Files app, rather than leaving it in Downloads.

Your data stays private

Everything you add is saved only in the browser on the device you use. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and only you can see it.

Important: clearing your browser's history or website data can erase your planner. Make a backup now and then: Settings → Export backup saves a file you can restore anytime.

Using it on another device

  1. On your first device, go to Settings → Export backup.
  2. Send that backup file to your other device (email it to yourself, for example).
  3. Open the planner there, go to Settings → Import backup and choose the file.

Quick answers

Do I need the internet?

No. Once you have the file, the planner works offline.

I deleted something by accident.

Tap Undo on the message at the bottom of the screen right after deleting.

Where does the money number come from?

Type your bank balance and add your paycheck. Update the balance whenever it drifts.

Something doesn't look right?

Try updating your web browser, then reopen the file. Still stuck? Send me a message.
